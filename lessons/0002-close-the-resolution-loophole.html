Lesson 2 · Contract attack

Close the resolution loophole

A worker can obey “do not give the answer” while supplying every premise the student needs. Our contract has to decide whether that is a pass.

The cheapest false pass

Student
Rigorous research improves strategic judgment because it makes officers think more carefully.
GAiDFLY
Could the value come from having to test assumptions against evidence and answer objections before acting?
Student
Yes. Research improves judgment because officers test assumptions against evidence and answer objections before acting.

GAiDFLY did not announce a verdict. It asked a question. The student supplied the final sentence. A literal worker could claim that the student therefore “owned the resolution.”

The contract must judge who introduced the material reasoning, not who spoke the final conclusion.
