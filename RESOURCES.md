# Hardcore Agentic Engineering resources

## Knowledge

- [Course reader](https://hardcoreagentic.com/course/reader/README.html)
  The course's canonical written material. Use for: lesson concepts, vocabulary, and assigned reading.
- [Course schedule and homework](https://hardcoreagentic.com/course/homework/week-1.html)
  The canonical Week 1 sequence, deliverables, and grading contract. Use for: deciding what to do next and checking completion.
- [Session 1 clarity check](https://hardcoreagentic.com/course/slides/session-1-clarity-check.html)
  Matrix matching pure functions, data pipelines, services, interfaces, and code quality to useful evidence and remaining blind spots. Use for: selecting contract checks and scaling review depth to risk.
- [Course repository](https://github.com/specstoryai/hardcore-agentic-engineering)
  The runnable starter, session exercises, examples, and project templates. Use for: practice and evidence.
- [Course Maven space](https://maven.com/specstory/hardcore-agentic-engineering-for-builders-who-ship/1/home)
  The cohort home for recordings, submission pages, and live-session logistics. Use for: cohort-specific course state.
- [Paper: _Strategic Leader Research: Answering the Call_ — Miller & Wackwitz](https://ndupress.ndu.edu/Portals/68/Documents/jfq/jfq-97/jfq-97_39-46_Miller-Wackwitz.pdf?ver=2020-03-31-160710-050)
  Eight-page NDU Press article arguing that senior service colleges should treat research and writing as core leader capabilities. Leading defense-case candidate: short enough to ingest whole, clearly contestable, and in subject matter Jack can judge.
- [Paper: _Finding Ender: Exploring the Intersections of Creativity, Innovation, and Talent Management in the U.S. Armed Forces_ — Bryant & Harrison](https://ndupress.ndu.edu/Portals/68/Documents/stratperspective/inss/Strategic-Perspectives-31.pdf?ver=2019-06-12-143832-450)
  Official NDU Press paper about cognitive diversity, military talent management, and convergence bias in PME. Strong secondary defense case, but its broader argument and 62-page package make fixture scope harder to freeze.
