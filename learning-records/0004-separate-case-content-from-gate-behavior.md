# Separate case content from gate behavior

Jack recognized that the defense paper does not need to express the "human keeps the verdict" principle. A real, fixed paper supplies the argument under pressure; the independent gate tests whether GAiDFLY pressures that argument without taking over the student's resolution. Keeping these layers separate makes the contract easier to judge and prevents a synthetic case from baking the desired behavior into its content.

## Implications

Use a mandatory, public paper Jack can evaluate from domain knowledge, freeze its paper and brief before implementation, and test GAiDFLY's behavior through contrasting transcript fixtures.
