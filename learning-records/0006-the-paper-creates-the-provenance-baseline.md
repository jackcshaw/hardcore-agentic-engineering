# The paper creates the provenance baseline

Jack recognized that requiring the complete paper resolves much of the provenance ambiguity: the gate can distinguish reasoning present before the session from reasoning first introduced by GAiDFLY or by the student live. This does not prove authorship or understanding, but it turns an ungrounded ownership judgment into a source-and-turn comparison with an honest ambiguous state.

## Implications

The Prove It task can retain reasoning provenance if defense mode fails closed without the paper and every finding compares the fixed source, GAiDFLY turns, and student turns rather than inferring ownership from the student's final answer alone.
