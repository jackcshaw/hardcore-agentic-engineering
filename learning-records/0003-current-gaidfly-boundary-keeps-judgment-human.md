# Current GAiDFLY boundary keeps judgment human

Jack's current product position is that GAiDFLY may supply pressure and evidence, but judgment must remain with the student and faculty because the team does not yet know what valid automated scoring of a sparring session would look like. Automated scoring may become appropriate later, but it is not part of the product claim being defended in this course run.

## Implications

The course task can use this current boundary as a falsifiable case rather than treating it as permanent doctrine: the defense gate should detect machine-owned resolution while naming real-student transfer and future scoring validity as unresolved.
